Good to know

A match is the closest colour in the other brand. It is a guide, not an exact copy, because every maker dyes its own shades.

Where it all comes from. DMC colours were measured by us from real thread on our own DMC colour card. Madeira colours come from Madeira’s own printed colour card. Anchor and Cosmo colours come from published charts and pictures, so they are less exact. Sullivans has no colours yet, so it is shown in its DMC colour. The matches come from each maker’s own chart for Madeira and Sullivans, and from several published charts for Anchor and Cosmo. We check every match against the colours and leave out any that are clearly wrong. Anything marked as our own suggestion is our closest fit by colour. Screens differ, so check against real thread where you can.

This is an independent guide. The Jolly Makery is not connected to any thread maker, and the brand names belong to their owners.
