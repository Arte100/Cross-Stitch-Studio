Good to know

A match is the closest colour in the other brand. It is a guide, not an exact copy, because every maker dyes its own shades.

Where the colours come from. DMC: measured by us from real thread on our own DMC colour card. Madeira: read from the printed colour boxes of the Madeira Mouliné colour card, 2024. Anchor: read from the coloured charts of Country Threads and Goblen ART, which agree closely. Cosmo: read from the pictures on the Wyldwood Creative comparison page, for about three in four Cosmo threads. Sullivans: we have no colours yet, so each pair is shown in its DMC colour. Colours read from a printed card or a picture are less exact than measured thread, and screens differ, so check against real thread where you can.

Where the matches come from. Sullivans: the Sullivans USA conversion chart. Madeira: the Madeira Mouliné colour card, 2024. Anchor: cross-checked between the charts of Country Threads, Lazy May Sewing Club and Cyberstitchers. Where they differ, the other numbers are listed as “Also suggested”. Cosmo: the Snuggly Monkey chart, which the Wyldwood Creative chart largely confirms. Sullivans is confirmed by the Cyberstitchers chart. Published charts contain slips, so we check every match against the colours above and leave out any that are clearly the wrong colour. Where that leaves a colour with no published match, we suggest the closest thread by colour and say so. Where a published match looks clearly off by colour, we keep it and name the thread that looks closer.

This is an independent guide. The Jolly Makery is not connected to any thread maker, and the brand names belong to their owners. Spotted a mistake? Please tell us on the feedback page and we will put it right.
