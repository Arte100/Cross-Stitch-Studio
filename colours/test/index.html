Good to know

A match is the closest colour in the other brand. It is a guide, not an exact copy, because every maker dyes its own shades.

The DMC colours on this page were measured by us from real thread on our own DMC colour card. DMC is the only brand we have measured so far, so each pair is shown in the colour of its DMC thread. Screens also differ, so check against real thread where you can.

Where the matches come from. Sullivans: the Sullivans USA conversion chart. Madeira: the Madeira Mouliné colour card, 2024. Anchor: cross-checked between the charts of Country Threads, Lazy May Sewing Club and Cyberstitchers. Where they differ, the other numbers are listed as “Also suggested”. Published charts contain slips, so we check every match against our measured colours and leave out any that are clearly the wrong colour. Cosmo: the Snuggly Monkey chart. J&P Coats: the Cyberstitchers chart, which uses the older J&P Coats numbers.

This is an independent guide. The Jolly Makery is not connected to any thread maker, and the brand names belong to their owners. Spotted a mistake? Please tell us on the feedback page and we will put it right.
