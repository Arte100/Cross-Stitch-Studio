About us

The Jolly Makery is run by two people who like making things. One of us stitches, and makes bracelets and keyrings. The other is an engineer by background, who took on building the tools as a challenge and as a way to learn how the craft world works.

We started with a cross stitch pattern designer, because we wanted one that was simple, free and worked on a phone. More tools for other crafts will follow.

Everything here is free to use. There is no account to create and there are no adverts.
